FAQ

What if we find the candidate ourselves in Scout?

No placement fee. Candidates you find and hire through Scout are yours.

When does a placement fee apply?

Only when we introduce the candidate, invite them to apply, or run the interviews. The fee is 15%, $700 of each month paid credits toward it (up to 25% of the fee), and every Direct Hire has a 60-day replacement guarantee.

What if a candidate we introduced applies to you directly later?

The fee still applies if you hire them within 12 months of our first introduction or invite. Scout timestamps every introduction, so there's no question who came first.

How many jobs can be open at once?

Active search on up to 3 open jobs at a time, plus a standing bench for up to 10 key seats.

Do we have to take the live first-round call?

No. Meet only the top 2–3 from the ranked shortlist, or we run a short live call first.

Can we use you for Temp or Payrolling too?

Yes. Temp, Contract, Temp-to-Hire, and Payrolling are billed at our standard rates, including if you already have the person.
