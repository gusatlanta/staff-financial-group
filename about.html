About Staff Financial Group

A boutique finance and accounting recruiting firm built on expertise, relationships, and results — serving Atlanta and the Southeast since 2005.

Our Story

Built Around One Simple Belief

Great organizations are built by great finance teams. Staff Financial Group exists to help both sides of that equation find each other — faster, smarter, and with more confidence than a generalist staffing firm can offer.

Finance and accounting is our core specialty, because we believe deep expertise produces dramatically better outcomes. When you call us, you're speaking with someone who knows the difference between a consolidating Controller and a hands-on one — and who knows exactly which candidates belong in each seat.

We know how critical every hire is — especially now, with so many of our clients planning for succession.

Headquartered in Atlanta, we serve clients and candidates throughout the Southeast and beyond, placing professionals at every level — from Staff Accountants to Chief Financial Officers.

Why Staff Financial Group

Finance Recruiting Done Differently

Finance and accounting is our specialty — and that focus changes everything.

Finance & Accounting Specialists

Finance and accounting is our core. We understand the technical requirements, career paths, certifications, and compensation norms that generalist firms guess at.

Deep Atlanta Network

Over 20 years of relationships with Atlanta's finance community — CFOs, Controllers, FP&A leaders, and the candidates they want to hire. We know who's looking and who should be.

Fast Shortlists

We don't let searches sit. With an active, pre-vetted candidate pipeline, we can deliver qualified candidates faster than firms that start from scratch on every search.

Custom Screening Questionnaires

For every search, we build a screening questionnaire with your input. Every résumé we submit comes with the candidate's answers — so your questions are answered up front, saving you time when you review candidates.

Video Introductions

We invite every candidate to film an optional video introduction. Candidates love the chance to showcase their experience — and clients love seeing how they come across before the first call. Over 1,000 candidates have already filmed one.

Replacement Guarantee

Every direct hire placement is backed by our replacement guarantee. If a candidate leaves within the guarantee period, we replace them — at no additional fee.

Market Intelligence

Salary benchmarks, candidate expectations, hiring timelines — we track the Atlanta finance market closely and share that intelligence freely. You'll never make an offer blind.

EOR Back-Office

We handle payroll, taxes, workers comp, and compliance for every contract and temp placement through our NorthPoint EOR infrastructure. One bill rate, zero HR overhead.

Example: Screening Questionnaire

Built around one client's system implementation

A client running five different systems wanted to select and implement one new system — positioning the company for its next 10 years of growth. Implementation experience was the key skill for the role, so we built their screening questionnaire around it. Every résumé arrived with the candidate's answers about the system selections and implementations they'd led, so the client could see who had that experience before the first call.

Our Values

What Guides Every Search

We hold ourselves to the same standard we expect from the candidates we represent — honesty, rigor, and a genuine commitment to long-term outcomes over short-term transactions.

  • Rigorous vetting — we never send a candidate we wouldn't personally vouch for
  • Honest communication — no spin, no over-promising, even when it's not what you want to hear
  • Long-term relationships — we measure success in years of repeat business, not individual placements
  • Market transparency — comp data, talent availability, and hiring reality shared up front
  • Responsiveness — you'll never wait days for an update; we treat your timeline like our own
