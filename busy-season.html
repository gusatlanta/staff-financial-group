Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Direct Hire and Contract?

Direct Hire means we find a tax professional for you to bring onto your firm’s team. Contract means we place someone for a set period — often to cover busy season — without making a lasting hire. You can start with either, or look at both, depending on the roles you need.

Do you place international candidates?

Yes. We place US-based or international tax talent. Every candidate is vetted, whether they work on-site, remotely from metro Atlanta, or from farther away.

How fast can you send candidates?

We move with the season in mind and send a fast, curated shortlist of five vetted candidates. Timing depends on the role and what you need. Tell us the seats you’re hiring for and we’ll talk through timing for your search.

What roles do you place for busy season?

Tax preparers, tax seniors, and tax managers. If you need a mix of those roles, or a blend of Direct Hire and Contract, tell us in your first email.
