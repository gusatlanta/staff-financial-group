Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Cumming, GA

Cumming is the seat of Forsyth County, one of the fastest-growing counties in Georgia, and its finance hiring follows that growth: homebuilders, contractors, medical groups and service companies that are adding their first or second accounting hire. Staff Financial Group recruits project accountants, full-charge bookkeepers, controllers and payroll staff for Cumming employers.

Get Started

Growth Companies in Forsyth County

Residential growth has brought homebuilders, trade contractors and property managers who need job costing, draw management, retainage tracking and work-in-progress schedules. Many are owner-led companies moving from an outside bookkeeper to an in-house accounting function for the first time.

Healthcare, dental and veterinary groups, plus retail and service businesses around the GA-400 exits and The Collection at Forsyth, round out demand. These employers often want one capable person who can run payroll, payables and the monthly close.

Roles We Fill for Cumming Employers

Construction and project accountants, full-charge bookkeepers, first in-house controllers, payroll administrators, and AP and AR staff. Temp-to-hire is a good fit for owners who want to see how someone works before committing, and we can payroll the person as Employer of Record during that period. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

Hiring at the North End of GA-400

Many Forsyth residents commute south on GA-400 every day, so a local role is a strong recruiting message here. Candidates come from Cumming, Suwanee, Buford, Dawsonville and north Alpharetta. Roles are mostly on site. Current pay ranges by title are in our 2026 Salary Guide.

How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.

Cumming Hiring FAQ

Do you recruit construction accountants in Cumming?

Yes. We screen for job costing, WIP schedules, draws and retainage, which builders and contractors here need.

Can you help us bring accounting in-house for the first time?

Yes. We recruit full-charge bookkeepers and first controllers who have set up in-house accounting before.

Is a local role attractive to Forsyth candidates?

Often, yes. Many experienced professionals commute down GA-400 and would rather work close to home.

Can we try a temp-to-hire arrangement?

Yes. You see the work first, and we act as Employer of Record until you hire.

Which areas do your Cumming candidates come from?

Cumming, Suwanee, Buford, Dawsonville and north Alpharetta.
