Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Cumberland, Atlanta

The Cumberland district at the I-75 and I-285 interchange is one of metro Atlanta's largest office markets, with corporate headquarters, shared-services centers and regional finance hubs packed around the Galleria and Circle 75 Parkway. Staff Financial Group recruits senior accountants, financial analysts, reporting specialists and controllers for Cumberland employers through Direct Hire and contract staffing.

Get Started

Corporate Finance and Shared Services Near The Battery

Cumberland's office towers house finance teams that run on scale: SEC and lender reporting, intercompany accounting, lease accounting and large AP operations. Employers here usually want specialists who have worked inside a bigger close calendar rather than generalists.

The growth around The Battery Atlanta and Truist Park has added mixed-use and hospitality employers whose finance needs include revenue audit and property-level accounting. We recruit for both the corporate towers and these newer businesses.

Specialist Roles We Recruit in Cumberland

SEC and external reporting accountants, consolidation and intercompany specialists, senior financial analysts supporting business-unit leaders, lease accounting staff, and AP managers who run shared-services teams. We also place interim controllers and contract reporting accountants for quarter-end and audit fieldwork. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

Getting to Cumberland: The I-75 and I-285 Factor

Traffic at the interchange shapes who will accept an offer. Most candidates come from Smyrna, Vinings, Marietta, Kennesaw and the west side of Sandy Springs. Many Cumberland employers offer hybrid schedules to offset the commute, and we confirm those terms up front. See our 2026 Salary Guide for current ranges by title.

How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.

Cumberland Hiring FAQ

Do you recruit SEC reporting accountants for Cumberland headquarters?

Yes. We screen for SEC filing, technical accounting and audit committee reporting experience.

Can you staff a shared-services AP team in the Galleria area?

Yes. We place AP managers and contract AP staff, and we can payroll contract workers as Employer of Record.

Do Cumberland employers hire hybrid?

Many do because of the I-75 and I-285 commute. We confirm each role's schedule with candidates before interviews.

Is Cumberland part of Atlanta or Cobb County?

The district sits in Cobb County with Atlanta mailing addresses, next to Smyrna and Vinings. We recruit across that whole area.

Can you provide an interim controller during a leadership change?

Yes. Interim controllers can step in on contract while a Direct Hire search runs.

How do I begin a Cumberland search?

Submit the Get Started form and a recruiter will contact you about the role and timeline.
