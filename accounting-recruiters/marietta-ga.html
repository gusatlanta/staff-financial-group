Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Marietta, GA

As the Cobb County seat, Marietta mixes public-sector finance, aerospace and manufacturing suppliers, healthcare groups and a large base of family-owned companies around the Square and East Cobb. Staff Financial Group recruits controllers, cost accountants, governmental accountants and staff accountants for Marietta employers on a Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire basis.

Get Started

Government, Aerospace Suppliers and Family-Owned Companies

Near Dobbins Air Reserve Base and along the I-75 corridor, aerospace and defense suppliers need accountants who understand job costing, contract compliance and indirect rate calculations. Government and education employers in and around the county seat need governmental and fund accounting experience.

Around Marietta Square and in East Cobb, many employers are second- or third-generation family companies where the controller is the senior finance person. Those searches call for someone who can own the books, manage the outside CPA relationship and modernize systems without disrupting the business.

Roles We Recruit for Marietta Employers

Controllers and accounting managers for family-owned companies, cost and project accountants for manufacturers and contractors, governmental and fund accountants, staff accountants and bookkeepers moving up, and payroll specialists. Recent graduates from the Kennesaw State University Marietta campus often fill entry roles, and we can include early-career candidates in a search. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

The Marietta Commute: I-75, the Loop and East Cobb

Candidates come from Kennesaw, Acworth, Smyrna, Powder Springs and East Cobb. East Cobb residents often prefer roles that avoid the I-75 merge, while west-side candidates reach the Square easily. Most Marietta finance roles are on site or lightly hybrid. Current pay ranges are listed in our 2026 Salary Guide.

How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.

Marietta Hiring FAQ

Do you recruit accountants with government contract experience in Marietta?

Yes. For aerospace and defense suppliers we screen for job costing, contract compliance and indirect rate experience.

Can you find a controller for a family-owned Marietta company?

Yes. We look for controllers who can run the books end to end, work with your outside CPA and improve systems at a pace that fits the business.

Do you place governmental accountants?

Yes. We screen for fund accounting and public-sector reporting experience.

Can you include recent graduates in a Marietta search?

Yes. For staff accountant and AP roles we can include early-career candidates alongside experienced ones.

Do you cover East Cobb employers?

Yes. East Cobb is part of our Marietta coverage, and we screen candidates for commute fit across Cobb County.
