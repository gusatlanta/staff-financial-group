Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Duluth, GA

Duluth's position on I-85 in Gwinnett County makes it a base for distributors, logistics operators and U.S. offices of international companies, with a large Korean-American business community along Pleasant Hill Road and Satellite Boulevard. Staff Financial Group recruits AP and AR specialists, staff and senior accountants, controllers and bilingual finance staff for Duluth employers.

Get Started

Distribution, International Offices and the Gas South District

Warehouses and distribution centers along the I-85 and Peachtree Industrial corridors need accountants comfortable with high invoice volume, freight and landed cost, and inventory reconciliations. U.S. subsidiaries of overseas parents need reporting to a foreign headquarters, intercompany accounting and transfer pricing support.

The Gas South District, with its arena and convention center, adds event and hospitality finance work. Across all of these, bilingual English and Korean finance candidates are a frequent request, and we can make language a screening requirement.

Duluth Roles in Demand

AP and AR specialists for high-volume operations, intercompany and reporting accountants for international subsidiaries, controllers for distributors, bilingual English and Korean staff accountants, and payroll specialists. Contract help is common for audit preparation and parent-company reporting deadlines. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

Commuting on I-85 and Pleasant Hill Road

Duluth draws from Lawrenceville, Suwanee, Norcross, Johns Creek and Buford. Pleasant Hill Road and the I-85 interchanges can be slow at rush hour, so candidates often weigh exact office location. Most roles are on site. See our 2026 Salary Guide for current ranges.

How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.
