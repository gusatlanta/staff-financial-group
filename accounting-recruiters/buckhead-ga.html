Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Buckhead, Atlanta

Buckhead's towers along Peachtree Road are home to investment managers, private equity firms, family offices, real estate sponsors and professional firms, and their finance hiring reflects it. Staff Financial Group recruits fund accountants, CFOs, controllers, tax professionals and financial analysts for Buckhead employers, with Direct Hire, interim and contract options.

Get Started

Investment, Real Estate and Family Office Finance

Between Lenox Square, Phipps Plaza and Buckhead Village, many employers are small teams managing large balance sheets. A fund accountant may own capital calls, waterfall calculations and investor reporting; a family office controller may cover entity bookkeeping, tax coordination and personal financial statements for principals.

Real estate owners and developers headquartered here need property and partnership accounting, while law and advisory firms need partner-level billing and trust accounting. These roles reward discretion and precision, and we screen for both.

Buckhead Searches We Handle

Fund and private equity accountants, portfolio-company CFOs and VPs of Finance, family office controllers, real estate and partnership accountants, tax managers with pass-through entity experience, and senior financial analysts supporting deal teams. Many Buckhead searches are confidential replacements, and we run them that way. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

Working in Buckhead: MARTA, GA-400 and Office Expectations

Buckhead is reachable by the MARTA Red Line at the Buckhead and Lenox stations and by GA-400, which widens the candidate pool to Brookhaven, Sandy Springs, Midtown and North Fulton. Investment and family office roles are more often fully in the office than corporate roles elsewhere in the metro. Ranges by title are in our 2026 Salary Guide.

How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.

Buckhead Hiring FAQ

Do you recruit fund accountants for Buckhead private equity firms?

Yes. We screen for capital call, distribution and waterfall experience, plus familiarity with fund administrator relationships.

Can you run a confidential CFO search in Buckhead?

Yes. Confidential Direct Hire searches are common here, and we present candidates without disclosing your company until you approve.

Do you place family office controllers?

Yes. We look for candidates who have handled multi-entity books, tax coordination and principal reporting with discretion.

Is MARTA a realistic commute for Buckhead roles?

For many candidates, yes. The Buckhead and Lenox stations on the Red Line serve the Peachtree Road corridor, and we ask about transit during screening.

Can you cover a tax season gap for a Buckhead firm?

Yes. Contract tax staff can support filing season, and we handle payroll as Employer of Record.

How do I start a Buckhead search?

Complete the Get Started form and a recruiter will follow up confidentially.
