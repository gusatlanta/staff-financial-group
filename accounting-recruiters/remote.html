Remote Accounting & Finance Recruiters

Not every finance role needs a desk in Atlanta. Staff Financial Group recruits remote accountants, FP&A analysts, controllers and tax professionals for companies that hire across the United States, and helps Atlanta employers widen their search beyond the metro.

Get Started

When a Remote Finance Hire Makes Sense

Roles with clear deliverables work well remotely: FP&A modeling, revenue and reporting accounting, tax preparation and review, and project work such as ERP implementations and audit support. Close-heavy roles can work remotely too when the company's systems are in the cloud and its approval workflows are digital.

Remote hiring widens the candidate pool for hard-to-fill specialties such as technical accounting, SEC reporting and multistate tax, where the right person may not live within driving distance of your office.

Remote Roles We Recruit

Remote FP&A analysts and managers, revenue and technical accountants, SEC reporting specialists, multistate tax accountants, fractional controllers and CFOs, and contract accountants for implementations, clean-ups and audit preparation. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

Payroll, Equipment and Time Zones

For contract and temp-to-hire roles, we act as Employer of Record, handling payroll, payroll taxes, workers compensation and W-2 reporting. We confirm which states we can payroll in at the start of each search. We also confirm time-zone coverage, equipment and data-security expectations with candidates before they interview. How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.

Remote Hiring FAQ

Can you hire remote accountants outside Georgia?

Yes, for Direct Hire roles. For contract roles we payroll as Employer of Record in the states we support, which we confirm at the start of the search.

Which finance roles work best remotely?

FP&A, reporting, revenue accounting, tax and project work tend to work well. Roles that need daily in-person approvals or physical documents are harder.

Do remote candidates record a video intro?

Every candidate is invited to record an optional video intro, so you can get a sense of communication style before the first call.

Can we start with a remote contractor and hire later?

Yes. Temp-to-hire works remotely, and conversion is straightforward.

How do you handle time zones?

We confirm working hours and overlap with your team during screening.

How do I start a remote search?

Use the Get Started form and choose remote as the location.
