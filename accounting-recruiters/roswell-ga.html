Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Roswell, GA

Roswell's employers range from healthcare and medical practices near Holcomb Bridge Road to professional firms in the Mansell Road office corridor and small businesses around Historic Roswell. Staff Financial Group recruits staff and senior accountants, healthcare finance staff, controllers and part-time or contract support for Roswell companies.

Get Started

Healthcare, Professional Services and Main Street Businesses

Healthcare is a large part of Roswell's employment base, so many finance searches involve physician practice accounting, patient billing oversight and revenue cycle reporting. Professional firms along Mansell Road, on the Roswell and Alpharetta line, need accountants for engagement billing and multi-office reporting.

Around Canton Street and the historic district, restaurants, retailers and service businesses often need an experienced bookkeeper or a part-time controller rather than a full department. We place contract and part-time finance help for those situations.

Roswell Roles We Fill

Healthcare accountants and practice finance managers, senior accountants for professional firms, part-time and fractional controllers, full-charge bookkeepers, and payroll administrators. Contract help is common for practice acquisitions, system changes and year-end catch-up. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

Holcomb Bridge, GA-400 and the Chattahoochee Crossing

Roswell draws candidates from Alpharetta, Sandy Springs, Marietta and East Cobb. The Chattahoochee River crossings and Holcomb Bridge Road traffic can make cross-county commutes longer than the map suggests, so we confirm travel tolerance early. Most roles here are on site or hybrid. Ranges by title appear in our 2026 Salary Guide.

How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.

Roswell Hiring FAQ

Do you recruit accountants for medical practices in Roswell?

Yes. We screen for practice accounting, revenue cycle reporting and payer reconciliation experience.

Can you provide a part-time controller for a small Roswell business?

Yes. We place part-time and fractional finance help, and we can payroll that person as Employer of Record.

Do you cover the Mansell Road office corridor?

Yes. We recruit for employers on both the Roswell and Alpharetta sides of Mansell Road.

Can you help with a backlog after an acquisition?

Yes. Contract accountants can clean up and integrate books while you plan the long-term team through a Direct Hire search.

Where do your Roswell candidates live?

Mostly in Roswell, Alpharetta, Sandy Springs, Marietta and East Cobb.

How do I begin a Roswell search?

Fill out the Get Started form and a recruiter will contact you.
