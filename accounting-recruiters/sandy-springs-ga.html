Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Sandy Springs, GA

Sandy Springs shares Perimeter Center with Dunwoody, one of the region's biggest concentrations of corporate offices, and it is home to the hospital cluster along Peachtree Dunwoody Road known as Pill Hill. Staff Financial Group recruits controllers, FP&A professionals, corporate accountants and healthcare finance staff for Sandy Springs employers.

Get Started

Perimeter Center Headquarters and the Medical Center

Perimeter Center's towers near the GA-400 and I-285 interchange hold national and regional headquarters, insurance and financial services operations, and franchise systems. Their finance teams run formal planning cycles, multi-location reporting and franchise or royalty accounting.

The Medical Center area brings hospital and physician group finance: cost reporting, revenue cycle analytics and departmental budgeting. City Springs and the Roswell Road corridor add smaller professional firms and real estate businesses that need hands-on accounting managers.

Sandy Springs Roles We Recruit

Corporate controllers and assistant controllers, FP&A managers, franchise and royalty accountants, hospital cost and reimbursement analysts, revenue cycle reporting staff, and accounting managers for real estate and professional firms. Interim help is common during system implementations and leadership transitions. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

MARTA Red Line and the 400/285 Interchange

The MARTA Red Line serves the area at the Medical Center, Dunwoody, Sandy Springs and North Springs stations, which lets employers recruit from Midtown, Buckhead and points south without a car commute. The 400/285 interchange is congested, so many Perimeter employers offer hybrid schedules. Pay ranges are in our 2026 Salary Guide.

How Hiring Works

Send the role through the Get Started form. We invite and screen qualified finance professionals, each of whom can record an optional video intro, and you hire through Direct Hire, contract or temp-to-hire.

Sandy Springs Hiring FAQ

Do you recruit hospital cost and reimbursement analysts in Sandy Springs?

Yes. We screen for cost reporting, reimbursement and departmental budgeting experience for the Medical Center area.

Can you staff FP&A for a Perimeter Center headquarters?

Yes. We place analysts and managers experienced with formal planning cycles and multi-location reporting.

Do you recruit franchise accountants?

Yes. We look for royalty, franchise fee and multi-unit reporting experience.

Is MARTA useful for Sandy Springs roles?

Yes. The Medical Center, Dunwoody, Sandy Springs and North Springs stations put many offices within reach of transit commuters.

Do you cover Dunwoody as part of Perimeter?

Yes. We recruit for employers on both the Sandy Springs and Dunwoody sides of Perimeter Center.
