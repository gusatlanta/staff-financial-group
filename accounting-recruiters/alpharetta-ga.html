Accounting & Finance Recruiters in Alpharetta, GA

Alpharetta employers hire finance people who can keep pace with growth: software and fintech teams that close books monthly for investors, and established regional offices that need steady accounting depth. Staff Financial Group recruits controllers, FP&A analysts, tax and staff accountants for Alpharetta companies through Direct Hire, contract and temp-to-hire placements.

Alpharetta's Finance Hiring: Windward, North Point and Downtown

The city calls itself the Technology City of the South, and its finance hiring shows it. Office parks along Windward Parkway and North Point Parkway hold software, payments and data companies whose accounting teams handle subscription revenue, multi-entity consolidations and audit readiness for outside investors.

Downtown Alpharetta and Avalon add a different profile: smaller headquarters and professional firms that want one strong generalist who can own the close, payroll oversight and board reporting. We recruit for both, and we tell you which candidates have worked in a venture-backed environment versus a mature regional office.

Roles We Fill Most Often in Alpharetta

Revenue accountants and senior accountants with SaaS or payments experience, FP&A analysts who build driver-based models, controllers ready to take a growing company through its first outside audit, and tax staff who understand multistate filings. For short projects such as an ERP migration or a quarter-end crunch, we place contract accountants who can start without a long ramp. See Direct Hire specialties, contract and interim talent and tax talent.

Commute and Work Style on the GA-400 Corridor

Most Alpharetta finance roles draw from Roswell, Johns Creek, Cumming and Milton, where candidates can reach the office without crossing I-285. Hybrid schedules are common for analyst roles, while controllers are usually expected on site for close week. We screen for commute fit early so offers are not lost to the drive. Alpharetta Hiring FAQ

Do you recruit for SaaS and fintech accounting teams in Alpharetta?

Yes. Many Alpharetta searches involve subscription revenue, deferred revenue schedules or payments reconciliations, and we screen candidates specifically for that experience.

Can you find a controller who has led a company through its first audit?

Yes. For growing Alpharetta companies preparing for an outside audit, we target controllers who have built audit-ready close processes before.

Do Alpharetta candidates expect hybrid work?

Many analyst and staff roles here are hybrid. We confirm each candidate's in-office expectations before you interview, so there are no surprises at offer stage.

Which nearby areas do your Alpharetta candidates come from?

Mostly the North Fulton and Forsyth side of GA-400: Roswell, Johns Creek, Milton and Cumming.

Can we start with a contract accountant and hire later?

Yes. Temp-to-hire lets you see the work first, and we act as Employer of Record during the contract period.

How do I start a search in Alpharetta?
