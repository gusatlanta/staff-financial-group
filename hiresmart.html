How it works

One example Senior Accountant search in metro Atlanta, from the first call to day one. The hiring manager, the company, and every candidate are illustrative. No real people or clients. Every candidate is invited to record a video intro. A video intro is included when they record one.

  1. Day 1

    You leave with a scorecard

    The intake call is about the seat, not a generic job post. You and the recruiter write what “good” means before anyone is invited: the close this person must own, the accounting they must already know, and what would take someone off the list.

    In this example, the scorecard says:

    • Owns a full month-end close, not one corner of it.
    • Has closed the books in industry, on GAAP.
    • Will be on site in metro Atlanta on the hybrid days you set.

    And the screening questions you wrote on the call:

    • How many closes have you owned in the last year, and for how many entities?
    • Which accounting system did you close in?
    • Which weekdays can you be in a metro Atlanta office?

  2. Day 1

    Your job meets a warm pipeline

    The same day, the role is posted and matched against people already in conversation with us about accounting work in metro Atlanta. You are not waiting on a cold post to fill itself. Anyone who already fits the scorecard is in the first pass.

  3. All month

    The right people get your questions

    Up to 300 candidates are invited to apply each month. Each invitation carries the screening questions from your scorecard, so an application comes back with answers, not a bare résumé.

    Every candidate is invited to record a video intro. When someone records one, you can watch it before you decide who to meet. A video intro is an invitation. Some people on the list will not have one, and the search does not wait on it.

    Invitations keep going after the first shortlist. That is how the bench for the next seat stays warm.

  4. Days 2–5

    You see answers before any meeting

    Screening answers are reviewed against the scorecard. A recruiter runs a phone screen with the people who fit. You are not on those first calls. By the time a name reaches you, the written answers and the phone screen are already done.

  5. Day 6

    Five ranked people, ready for you

    In this example the Fab Five is in your hands on day 6. Five people, ranked by fit to the scorecard you wrote on day 1. You spend your time on the top of the list. A video intro is included for a person who recorded one.

FAQ

Can I hire someone myself and pay no placement fee?

$0 traditional placement fee on qualifying Scout™ self-hires, including eligible LinkedIn Chrome extension-sourced applicants. Qualifying requirements apply.

Are job postings unlimited?

Yes. Start for Free includes unlimited job postings, auto-scoring, a salary benchmark, and the free community.

What is in the free community?

You become a member of our 100X Recruiting community: a private Skool community with live weekly hiring calls, replays, templates, and all of our hiring GPTs (job post writer, interview guides, offer letters, pay ranges).

Do I have to use StaffFinancial recruiters?

No. You can find people yourself in Scout™. Ask our recruiters to find them when you want a member Direct Hire.

Can you help with a hard-to-fill position?

Yes. The standard Direct Hire fee in our comparison is 22%. Members pay 15%. On $100,000 that is $22,000 versus $15,000. There is a 60-day replacement guarantee on every member Direct Hire.

Can I hire for more than one position?

Yes. Eligible accounting and finance job postings are unlimited, so more than one seat can be open.

Do you guarantee I will hire someone?

No. Candidate responses, hiring outcomes, and start dates are not guaranteed.

Is HireSmart™ a 12-month commitment?

The HireSmart™ rung is $995 per month on a 12-month membership, $11,940 for the year. Start for Free is $0.

Are Temp, Contract, Temp-to-Hire, and Payrolling included in the membership price?

No. Temp, Contract, Temp-to-Hire, payroll, and employer-of-record services are priced separately. Payrolling through our back office is for people your company sourced. Onboarding and eligibility apply.

Can I see Scout™ AI before I join?

Yes. Tell us in the Start for Free form and we'll walk you through it.
