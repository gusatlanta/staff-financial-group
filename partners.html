Join Our Partner Program

You spent your career building relationships. Put those relationships to work — you bring the hiring need, we do all the recruiting, and you get paid.

For CPAs, Executives & Anyone Whose Network Hires

You get the order. We fill it.

You get paid for every hour someone works.

You get the order

Hear "we need to hire" from someone in your network — and make the introduction.

We fill it

Our recruiters run the entire search — sourcing, screening, and placing. Contract, try-before-you-hire, or permanent.

You get paid

A referral fee on permanent hires, and a recurring share of every hour on contract — for the life of the assignment.

What You Earn

Simple, generous, recurring.

10%
Permanent placements

Your client hires permanent through us — you earn 10% of the placement fee we collect. Paid when we collect from the client.

25%
Contract net spread

Your client runs contract or try-before-you-hire workers — you earn 25% of the net spread on every hour they bill.

Recurring
While they work

Contract commissions aren't one-time. They keep paying as long as your client's workers keep billing. Build a book.

15th
Paid every month

Commissions are calculated on money actually received and paid on the 15th of the following month. Every dollar tracked for you.

Who This Is For

Anyone whose network hires.

CPAs and retired CPA partners. CFOs and Controllers. Healthcare and manufacturing executives. HR leaders, consultants, and retired executives — anyone whose network is always hiring. You never become a recruiter. You bring the hiring need and stay the relationship owner; our team fills the order.

How It Works

Four simple steps.

  1. Apply below — we review every partner personally and set you up.
  2. Hear "we need a Controller" (or a bookkeeper, a tax senior, a whole team) — send it in.
  3. Our recruiters fill the order — contract, try before you hire, or permanent.
  4. You're paid on the 15th of the following month — 10% of the fee on perm, 25% of net spread on contract, recurring while the worker bills.

Net spread = what the client pays, minus worker wages and employer burden (taxes, workers comp, benefits, compliance). Commissions are calculated on payments actually received; refunded or unpaid invoices are reversed. Self-referrals and companies already under agreement with us don't qualify. Earned commissions are never reduced.
